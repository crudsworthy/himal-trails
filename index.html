Questions, answered

The things most first-time trekkers ask us.

Do I need trekking experience?

Not for the easy and moderate routes. If you can walk for five to six hours a day, you can do Poon Hill or Mardi Himal.

What is the best time of year to trek?

March to May and October to November have the clearest skies and the most stable weather.

What is included in the price?

Guide, permits, lodging and meals on the trail. Flights and personal gear are not included.
