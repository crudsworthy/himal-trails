About Us

A small team of guides and trip planners from Nepal.

Our story

Himal Trails began as a few friends guiding visitors through the valleys they grew up in. We saw how often travellers booked large, rushed tours and missed the quiet side of the mountains: the tea houses, the villages and the people who live there.

Today we run small groups only, hire local guides and porters at fair wages, and keep our prices open so there are no surprises. Every route on this site is one our own team has walked many times.

What we stand for

Safety first

Trained guides, first-aid kits and a plan for altitude on every trek.

Small groups

No more than twelve people, so the trail feels like a trail.

Respect for places

We carry out what we carry in and spend with local businesses.
